Littlebit

Privacy Policy

Updated October 13, 2026 (version 1.4)

In short: your baby's records are stored on your own phone. If you use Family logging, records are also stored in your iCloud and can only be seen by the family members you invite. The developer cannot view your baby's records. We receive limited purchase history from Apple to manage and understand Littlebit Pro purchases. Littlebit doesn't require an account.

Data you record

Littlebit stores the information you enter: your baby's nickname, date of birth, sex, and the pregnancy week at birth if you add it, feeding, sleep, and diaper logs, pumping and milk stash records, solid food logs and reactions, temperature, medicine, vitamin, and symptom logs, activities such as tummy time and baths, teeth and potty training logs, moments and their photos, a pregnancy checklist, growth measurements, vaccines, and developmental milestones, plus your own habits (habits stay on this device only). This data is stored on your device and is never sent to the developer's server. Our purchase-notification server does not receive baby records.

Photos you add to moments are chosen by you in the system photo picker; Littlebit only receives the photos you pick. They are stored on your device, and in your iCloud if you use Family logging, never on our servers.

The weekly summary is worked out on your device. Where Apple Intelligence is available, its short opening sentence is written by Apple's on-device model; your records are not sent to Apple or to us for this.

If you use Share an update, the summary you choose is sent through the app you pick, such as WhatsApp. Littlebit does not send it anywhere by itself.

Family logging (iCloud)

This feature stays off until you invite a family member yourself. When you share, your baby's records are copied to your own iCloud (Apple's CloudKit service) and shared only with the people you invite. If you join someone else's family log, what you record is stored in the iCloud of the person who invited you.

This data is stored and transferred by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. As the developer, we cannot access the contents of your iCloud. You can stop sharing at any time in Settings > Family logging > Stop sharing (or Leave family).

Data we don't collect from the app

  • No account sign-up, email address, or phone number.
  • No in-app behavior analytics, tracking, or ads.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, notifications are scheduled directly on your device. No data is sent anywhere for this.

Littlebit Pro purchases

Apple processes payments. Apple's App Store Server Notifications send our server purchase events, including a transaction identifier, original transaction identifier, product, purchase and expiry dates, trial or refund status, price, currency, and storefront when available. We store these events and use them for purchase administration and sales reporting. We do not receive your card or bank details, name, email address, or your baby's records through these notifications. Purchase events are associated with a persistent transaction identifier; they are not used for advertising or tracking.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. For questions about stored purchase records or a deletion request, contact us using the address below.

PDF reports

Doctor reports are created on your device. A report is only shared if you choose to share it yourself, for example via WhatsApp or email.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time in the app under Settings > Delete all data, or by deleting the app from your device. If you have used Family logging, stop sharing first. The copy in iCloud can be deleted in iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

Littlebit is intended for parents and caregivers, not for use by children. Information about a child is entered only by the parents and stays on the parents' device, or in the parents' iCloud if Family logging is used.

Not a substitute for medical advice

Information in Littlebit is general and does not replace an examination or advice from a doctor, midwife, or other health professional.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
